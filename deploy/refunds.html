Cancellations & refunds

Draft for review. This text was prepared as a starting point and must be checked by a lawyer in the Maldives before YD Works relies on it.

Plans change on building sites. Here's how cancelling, rescheduling and refunds work.

Materials orders

  • Before a driver is assigned: cancel free in the app (Orders → Cancel).
  • After loading has started: call the counter on 992 0202. A delivery fee may apply.
  • Wrong or damaged goods: report within 24 hours of delivery with a photo; we'll replace or refund them.
  • Cement, cut steel and special-order items can't be returned once delivered, unless they are faulty.

Equipment bookings

  • Reschedule in the app any time before the driver starts loading, if the new time is free.
  • Cancel free before a driver is assigned. After that, a mobilisation fee may be charged if the machine has already left the yard.

Refunds

  • Card payments are refunded to the same card through Bank of Maldives, usually within 7–14 working days.
  • Bank transfers are refunded to the account they came from.
  • Cash payments are refunded at the counter or by bank transfer.

House-building projects

Cancellation and refunds for construction work follow your signed project contract.
