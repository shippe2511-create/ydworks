Privacy policy

Draft for review. This text was prepared as a starting point and must be checked by a lawyer in the Maldives before YD Works relies on it.

YD Works ("we") runs the yd-mv.com website, the YD Works customer app, the YD Driver app and the YD Works Console. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, email address and password (stored encrypted by our hosting provider).
  • Orders and bookings: what you order or hire, building-site addresses, notes, times, totals, invoices and payment status.
  • Location: saved site locations you choose to add. For drivers, the vehicle's location while they are online, so customers can follow deliveries.
  • Proof of delivery: photos of delivered goods, the receiver's name and signature.
  • Payments: bank-transfer slips you upload. Card payments are processed by Bank of Maldives (BML Connect); we never see or store your card number.
  • House-building projects: milestones, payment schedules and progress photos of your build.
  • Website quote requests: name, phone, site and the details you write.
  • App diagnostics: anonymous error reports and usage events (for example what people search for) so we can fix problems and stock what customers need.

How we use it

  • To take, price, deliver and invoice your orders and bookings.
  • To show you live delivery tracking and send order updates by app notification, WhatsApp, Viber or SMS (your choice in the app).
  • To keep tax records, which Maldivian law requires us to keep.
  • To prevent fraud and spam, and to improve our service.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that run the service for us: our hosting and database provider (Supabase), Bank of Maldives for card payments, Google Firebase and Apple for app notifications, Meta (WhatsApp), Rakuten Viber or Twilio when you choose those channels, and a map tiles provider. Drivers see the customer name, phone number and site for the job they are doing. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Account information is kept while your account is open. Invoices and order records are kept for as long as tax law requires, even after an account is deleted (they are anonymised). Diagnostics are kept for up to 12 months.

Your choices

  • Update your name, phone and sites in the app.
  • Choose or turn off update channels in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account) or by emailing yd@yd-mv.com. This removes your profile, saved sites and notifications; past invoices are kept anonymised.
  • Location access can be turned off in your phone's settings.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by role, and staff actions in the Console are recorded in an activity log.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults; we don't knowingly collect information from children under 18.
